FastForge / What we do

A hackathon
you can hold.

The Possible Zone's (TPZ) annual Hackathon, FastForge, offers Boston area high school students the chance to engage in a mini physical consumer product launch, reflecting our six-month post-high school Ventures Launch program. Throughout the day, students will collaborate in teams with corporate volunteers and TPZ staff to research, ideate, design, prototype, and pitch the next hot consumer product.

The Hackathon adopts a science-fair format, with judges and investors circulating among the teams to hear pitches and ask questions. At the end of the day, the top three teams will present in the finals, where the full audience will vote to choose the winner.

Adding to the excitement, recent Founding Associates in our Ventures Launch program will showcase their recently launched Kickstarter products, inspiring high school students by demonstrating what's possible at The Possible Zone!

About TPZ Ventures
TPZ Ventures is the revenue-generating arm of The Possible Zone, giving Boston-area high school and post-high school youth hands-on business experience. Launch, its six-month physical consumer product incubator, develops TPZ alumni and other young adults into co-founders. Each Founding Associate researches, ideates, and brings a new consumer product to life in a competitive, collaborative sprint to $1,000,000 in gross sales — learning product management, digital marketing, and e-commerce operations along the way.
